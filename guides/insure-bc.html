⚓ SWAG IDEAS

1. 🗺️ Arrival & “All Hands on Deck”

Make check-in feel like the beginning of an expedition. These opening pieces establish the pirate theme immediately while keeping the execution sophisticated, useful, and appropriate for a leadership audience.

Product Description
Weathered Captain Trucker Cap A premium washed-navy or charcoal cap featuring a small embroidered compass, crossed-swords, or “All Hands on Deck” emblem. Keep the InsureBC logo intentionally subtle on the side or back so the piece feels like lifestyle merchandise attendees will genuinely wear after the summit.
Antique Brass Compass Keychain A weighty compass-inspired metal keychain engraved with “A New Course. A Familiar Captain.” The InsureBC mark can live discreetly on the reverse, transforming the event message into an elegant leadership keepsake.
Captain’s Log Hardcover Journal A deep navy or weathered-brown notebook with antique-gold compass detailing and understated InsureBC branding. A custom inside page can feature summit priorities, leadership notes, or “coordinates for the year ahead,” making the journal part of the working experience instead of simply another giveaway.

2. 🧭 Leadership Experience

These pieces become part of the two-day summit itself—functional enough for meetings, travel, and daily use while creating visual continuity across the leadership group.

3. 🏆 Recognition & Take-Home Treasure

Finish the summit with pieces that feel earned rather than simply handed out. A strong closing reveal turns the merchandise into a tangible reminder of the leadership experience.

Product Description
Leadership Doubloon Challenge Coin An antique-gold challenge coin featuring the 2026 summit crest on one side and an InsureBC leadership message on the other. Present it during the closing session rather than placing it in a welcome bag—the ceremony gives a compact product significantly more emotional value.
Captain’s Canvas Treasure Pouch A heavyweight cotton or canvas drawstring pouch printed with a small compass emblem and designed to hold the challenge coin alongside premium chocolates or locally inspired treats. It creates a playful treasure reveal while keeping the presentation polished and leadership-appropriate.
Voyager Waterproof Dry Bag A compact roll-top bag in navy or black with understated tonal branding. The nautical connection fits the summit naturally, while its real value comes afterward for boating, travel, gym gear, outdoor weekends, and everyday West Coast adventures.

✅ Implementation Tips

Build a Reveal Sequence

Avoid distributing everything at check-in. Start with the journal and compass, introduce another useful piece during the working sessions, and save the challenge coin and treasure pouch for the closing moment. The merchandise becomes part of the summit story.
Create One Summit Crest

Develop a custom “InsureBC Leadership Summit 2026” ship-and-compass emblem to carry most of the visual identity. Keep the corporate InsureBC logo secondary and subtle so the collection feels exclusive, wearable, and event-specific.
Turn Packaging Into Treasure

Use kraft textures, navy tissue, rope-style cord, antique-gold seals, and a map-inspired insert. Strategic packaging can make practical products feel fully custom without requiring every individual item to carry a premium price point.
