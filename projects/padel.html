Level 1 · Four-Week Block

Padel Strength Plan

Three gym days designed to build hip, core, calf, foot and upper-body strength without turning every session into another hard leg day.

3Gym days
4–6Weeks
6–7/10Effort

Posterior Chain + Push/Pull

Full body with controlled lower-body loading.

50–60 min
Day 1 progress 0 / 8 complete

Dead-Bug Heel Slide

3 × 6 each side

Keep the foot on the floor. Slide only as far as you can without the left lower back arching, rotating or “sticking.”

Goblet Squat

3 × 8

Use a comfortable stance with only slight turnout. Avoid a deep, wide sumo position or any front-of-hip pinch.

Romanian Deadlift

3 × 8

Push your hips backward and keep the weights close. Stop before you feel pulling or pinching under the front of the left hip.

Dumbbell Bench Press

3 × 8–10

Keep both feet comfortable and ribs controlled. Do not force a large lower-back arch.

Chest-Supported Row

3 × 8–10

Pull the elbows toward your hips while keeping your chest supported. Avoid shrugging the shoulders.

Glute Bridge

3 × 10–12

Pause for two seconds at the top. Finish through the glutes without arching through the lower back.

Straight-Knee Calf Raise

3 × 12–15

Move slowly and keep pressure through the heel and base of the big toe. Start with both legs if needed.

Cable/Band External Rotation

2 × 12–15 each arm

Keep the elbow against your side. Use light resistance and move slowly—this is shoulder-control work.

Upper Body + Core

Minimal leg fatigue; padel shoulder support.

40–50 min
Day 2 progress 0 / 9 complete

Pallof Press

3 × 8 each direction

Press the handle forward and resist rotation. Keep your ribs and pelvis stacked.

Lat Pulldown / Assisted Pull-Up

3 × 8–10

Pull the elbows down without leaning far backward. Keep the neck relaxed.

Seated Dumbbell Shoulder Press

3 × 8–10

Use back support. Stop the set if you need to arch or rotate through the lower back.

Seated Cable Row

3 × 10

Sit tall and pull toward your lower ribs. Keep the shoulders away from your ears.

Incline Dumbbell Press / Push-Ups

2–3 × 8–12

Keep the shoulder blades controlled and avoid forcing the elbows excessively wide.

Face Pull

2–3 × 12–15

Pull toward eye level and finish with the hands apart. Keep the movement smooth.

Side Plank From Knees

3 × 20–30 sec each side

Keep your pelvis stacked rather than rolling backward. Stop if the left lower back catches.

Wrist Extension

2 × 12–15 each arm

Rest your forearm on a bench, palm down. Lift normally and lower slowly for about three seconds.

Optional Biceps + Triceps

2 × 10–12 each

Keep these controlled and stop well before failure. They are optional—not the priority of the program.

Single-Leg Control + Push/Pull

Hip stability, calves, feet and upper body.

50–60 min
Day 3 progress 0 / 8 complete

Short-Stance Split Squat

3 × 6–8 each leg

Keep the stance short. When the left leg is behind you, stop before the front of the left hip stretches or catches.

Supported Single-Leg RDL

3 × 8 each leg

Hold a wall or rack. Keep your pelvis facing forward and use a smaller range on the left if needed.

Low Lateral Step-Down

3 × 6–8 each leg

Use a low step and lower for about three seconds. Keep the knee over the middle of the foot.

One-Arm Cable Row

3 × 8–10 each side

Resist twisting as you row. Use a split or square stance—whichever keeps your hip and lower back comfortable.

Machine Chest Press / Landmine Press

3 × 8–10

Choose the version that lets you keep your pelvis and lower back quiet. Use the machine if standing causes compensation.

Bent-Knee Calf Raise

3 × 12–15

Keep the knee slightly bent throughout. Move through a comfortable range without provoking the left foot.

Suitcase Carry

3 × 30 sec each side

Walk slowly without leaning. Reduce the weight if the left lower back tightens or “sticks.”

Foot Tripod Hold

4 × 10 sec each foot

Maintain contact under the heel, base of the big toe and base of the little toe without curling your toes.

Pain and loading rules

Keep discomfort at 0–2/10. Reduce the range at 3/10. Stop for sharp groin pain, catching, limping or left lower-back “sticking.” You should be at your normal baseline by the next morning.

Move to Level 2 after 4–6 weeks when:

  • Walking no longer causes the painful push-off transition.
  • No lower-back catching during the strength sessions.
  • Two normal padel sessions do not create a multi-day flare.
  • Split squats and single-leg hinges feel controlled.
  • You are back to baseline the morning after training.
  • A small lying march is comfortable without compensation.
