The new target

You are already active enough. The goal now is better structure: fewer junk exercises, progressive strength, true Zone 2 cardio, and smarter decisions on low-recovery days.

Green WHOOPFull plan. Push main lifts at 1–2 RIR. Padel can be competitive.
Yellow WHOOPTrain, but cut 1–2 accessories. Keep padel technical or moderate.
Red / high RHRRecovery-only version. Walk, mobility, Pilates basics, light bike. No ego padel.

Progression rule

  • Main lifts: finish most working sets with 1–2 clean reps left. Add weight after you hit the top of the rep range on all sets.
  • Rehab/core: progress control first, range second, load last. No sharp pain or next-day flare.
  • Zone 2: stay easy enough to talk in full sentences. Do not turn it into another hard session.
  • Padel: third weekly session is allowed only when recovery is green and sleep was decent.
